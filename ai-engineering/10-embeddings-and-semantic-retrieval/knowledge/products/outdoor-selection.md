# Outdoor selection guide

This authored demo guide compares seeded catalog descriptions across categories.
It is not a manufacturer manual. Obtain current details, price and availability
from the Java commerce APIs.

## Packing for wet weather

The Lightweight Rain Jacket is described as a packable water-resistant shell
with taped seams and an adjustable hood. It is a candidate for a customer asking
for a layer that packs away and offers protection from light rain. Water-resistant
does not imply protection in all weather. No waterproof test rating is supplied.

## Clothes for travel

The Merino Wool T-Shirt is described as a breathable merino blend for travel,
layering and daily wear. The Everyday Cotton Hoodie is described as a soft,
heavyweight cotton hoodie with a relaxed fit. A customer prioritizing a breathable
travel layer may prefer to investigate the merino blend; a customer seeking a
heavyweight casual layer may prefer to investigate the hoodie. Exact fiber
percentages, sizes and washing temperatures are not provided here.

## Running on uneven paths

The Trail Running Shoes are described as having a grippy outsole and protective
toe guard. These details are relevant to a customer wanting footwear for uneven
outdoor paths. The guide does not establish waterproofing or guarantee that the
shoes fit an individual customer's feet.
