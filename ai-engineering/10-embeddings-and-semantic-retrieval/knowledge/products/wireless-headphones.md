# Wireless Headphones

This learning guide concerns the seeded SoundForge Wireless Headphones. It is
authored demo guidance, not an official manufacturer manual. Confirm current
product details through the Java catalog API.

## Description and selection

The seeded description states that the headphones provide wireless audio,
active noise reduction and long battery life. These features make them worth
considering when a customer wants listening with less surrounding noise or fewer
audio cables. Active noise reduction should not be described as complete silence.

## Getting started and care

For a real product, consult its supplied instructions before pairing or charging.
Keep the headphones dry and clean them gently; do not assume a waterproof rating.
This demo source does not specify a pairing-button sequence or charging voltage.

## Specification limits

Exact battery hours, Bluetooth version, multipoint support, microphone quality
and codec support are not specified. This guide cannot confirm whether a laptop
