# Returns policy

These are fictional demo terms used to test retrieval. They are not legal advice,
real store terms, or a statement of statutory consumer rights. The application
does not yet process return requests or refunds.

## Return window and condition

Under this demo policy, customers may request a change-of-mind return within
30 calendar days of delivery. Items should be complete, clean and undamaged,
with included accessories. Opening outer packaging by itself does not make an
item ineligible under these demo terms.

## Headphones and opened packaging

For the demo Wireless Headphones, opening the outer box is allowed for inspection
if the headphones remain clean, undamaged and complete with accessories. This
document does not define rules for sealed in-ear hygiene products or other
headphone models. Do not infer eligibility for those products from this example.

## Who pays for return shipping

For a change-of-mind return, the customer pays return postage under these demo
terms. For a confirmed faulty item or an incorrect item sent by the store, the
demo store covers approved return postage. Contact support for approval before
shipping the item; a chat answer does not approve a return.

## Refund timing

The demo policy estimates five to ten business days for a refund after receipt
and inspection of the returned item. Refund processing is not implemented, and
the assistant cannot confirm that an individual refund has been issued.
