# Shipping policy

These are fictional AI Commerce demo terms for learning, not a real carrier's
service commitment. They do not describe the status of an individual order.

## Processing and delivery estimates

For the demo store, order processing takes one to two business days after payment
confirmation. Standard delivery within Germany is estimated at three to five
business days after dispatch. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays.
Processing time and delivery time are separate; delivery estimates are not guarantees.

## Destinations

The demo shipping policy covers delivery within Germany. It does not specify
delivery times or charges for other countries. Ask support about unsupported destinations.

## Costs and tracking

The current demo checkout does not calculate a separate shipping charge. This
document does not promise a permanent free-shipping offer. An order status such
as confirmed is not proof that a parcel has shipped. Tracking links and exact
arrival dates are not implemented in the commerce application.
