# USB-C Hub

This is authored demo guidance for the seeded Portly USB-C Hub, not an official
manufacturer manual. Verify current product information through the Java API.

## Ports and purpose

The seeded description identifies a seven-port USB-C hub with HDMI, a card reader
and pass-through charging. It is a candidate for a customer wanting to connect
a display and memory card through fewer laptop ports.

## Compatibility checks

Having a USB-C connector does not by itself establish support for every hub
feature. Check the laptop documentation for display-output and charging support,
and check the actual hub specifications before purchasing. This source does not
specify supported operating systems, HDMI resolution or maximum charging wattage.

## Hub versus laptop stand

The USB-C Hub expands connections. The Laptop Stand changes the physical position
of a laptop for posture and airflow. A stand does not supply HDMI or card-reader
ports. These products address different desk needs and may be used together.
